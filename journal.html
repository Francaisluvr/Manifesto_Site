Permission to Play

Stories, inspiration, and creative adventures for makers who believe whimsy isn't optional—it's survival

Permission to Play is about loosening your grip, following curiosity, and letting your hands wander without a plan or a deadline. It’s about sewing for the joy of it, choosing colors just because they make you smile, and starting projects that don’t need to be optimized, shared, or explained. Here, play counts as progress. Imperfect counts as finished. And joy is a perfectly good reason to begin.
